# ResearchFund

A decentralized scientific research funding platform that incentivizes quality research contributions and democratizes funding allocation.

## Overview

ResearchFund is a blockchain-based platform that enables:
- Researchers to register their contribution credits
- Merit-based funding allocation for research projects
- Transparent distribution of research grants

## Features

- **Contribution Tracking**: Record and track research contribution credits
- **Funding Allocations**: Automatic allocation distribution to encourage quality research
- **Proportional Funding**: Research funding proportional to contribution level
- **Coordination Controls**: Administrative oversight for funding integrity

## Functions

- `establish-fund`: Initialize the system with a fund coordinator
- `contribute-research`: Record research contribution credits
- `distribute-allocations`: Calculate and distribute funding allocations
- `submit-proposal`: Allow researchers to submit proposals and claim funding

## Getting Started

1. Deploy the contract to your blockchain
2. Establish the fund with a trusted coordinator
3. Researchers can begin registering their contribution credits
4. Periodically distribute allocations to encourage ongoing research

## Security

The system includes permission controls to ensure only authorized coordinators can perform sensitive operations like allocation distribution.
